Why book a shoot

The island photographs itself. Almost.

You just need someone who knows where to stand.

The dunes at Maspalomas. The harbour at Puerto Mogán. Las Canteras at six in the evening. Same beach, different hour, completely different picture.

Hand your phone to a stranger and you get proof you were here. Sun in your eyes, someone half out of the frame. You keep it anyway, but it never looks like the day felt.

I've lived here for more than eleven years. I know which beach turns gold in the evening and which corner keeps the wind off you.

Before you book

Good questions

What should we wear?

Soft colours that go together without matching. Light fabrics like cotton or linen. Skip logos and busy prints. For the dunes or the rocks, sandals with some grip.

What time of day is best?

Early morning or the last hours before sunset. The light is softer and warmer, and everyone looks more like themselves in it.

We have small children. Is that OK?

Of course. Bring water and a snack, and we take a break whenever they need one.

Can we choose the location?

Yes. Or tell me the feeling you want, relaxed, dramatic, colourful, and I'll suggest the place. For spots further than about 30 minutes away, a small travel supplement may apply. I'll tell you before you book.

When and how do we get the photos?

Within 12 to 72 hours, in a digital gallery you can download straight to your phone. With The Film, the 60-second film comes with it.

In which language do we talk?

Yours, if it's English, Spanish, Dutch or Portuguese.
